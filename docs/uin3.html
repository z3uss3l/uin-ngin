3. Core Design Decision

UIN intentionally stops before interpretation.

  • Objects exist because they are declared
  • Relations exist because they are stated
  • No inference, no guessing, no confidence scores

4. Role of AI in This Architecture

  • AI can generate UIN
  • AI can consume UIN
  • AI can enrich UIN via plugins
  • AI is never required for validity

5. Why This Matters

  • Long-term archival & forensic use
  • Offline & sovereign systems
  • Interoperability across decades
  • No vendor or model lock-in
